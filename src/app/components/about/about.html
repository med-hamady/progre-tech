About Proger Technology

A Mauritanian tech company that actually ships

We're a team of four engineers based in Nouakchott who build software, design networks, and solve technical problems for businesses in Mauritania and across West Africa.

We started Proger Technology in 2020 because we saw too many local businesses struggling with outdated systems, unreliable infrastructure, and vendors who overpromise and underdeliver. We decided to do things differently: understand the problem first, pick the right tools, build it properly, and be there when something breaks.

Our Mission

Help businesses in Mauritania and across Africa get the technology they need to operate, grow, and compete — without the headaches that usually come with IT projects.

Our Vision

Be the company people think of first when they need something built in Mauritania. The team that delivers on time, writes code that works, and picks up the phone when you call.
